# Style: Breaking News

A parody of a frantic local news broadcast, played completely straight. LIVE badges, condensed all-caps headlines, lower-third name plates and a scrolling ticker. The humor comes from treating a small or silly story with the urgency of national news.

## Use it for
- Announcements, launches, milestones and "this just happened" stories.
- Parody and pop-culture content, especially for Pakistani and South Asian audiences who know the format well.
- Introducing people, players or customers one at a time (name plates do this naturally).

## Avoid it for
- Genuinely serious or sensitive news, where the parody could read as mockery or misinformation.
- Calm, premium or trust-heavy brands (finance advisory, healthcare), unless the parody is the explicit point.

## Color tokens
| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #0B1F3A | Every scene (deep navy) |
| Primary text | #FFFFFF | Headlines, body on navy |
| Plate text | #0B1F3A | Text on white or yellow plates |
| Plate | #FFFFFF | Lower-third name plates |
| Inactive | #2C4A73 | Rank boxes for non-leaders, borders, footage card border |
| Alert accent | #E10600 | LIVE badge, BREAKING tag, the leader's rank box |
| Ticker | #FFC400 | Ticker band and the single highlighted word in a headline |

Accent rule: red is for alerts and the one most important item; yellow is for the ticker and at most one highlighted word per headline. Never put red text on navy (it fails contrast); red is always a background with white text.

## Type
- Display: Anton, all caps, for headlines and rank numbers.
- Text: Inter 500 and 700, for plates, ticker and labels.
- Labels and badges: Inter 700, all caps, letter spacing 0.12em.
- Numbers: tabular figures.

## Layout
- Left-aligned on an 72px margin at 1080 width.
- Fixed zones that persist across scenes: badge row at the top of the safe area, headline below it, content in the middle, ticker pinned just above the bottom safe line.
- Name plates stack vertically with 20px gaps: a square rank box on the left, a wide plate on the right.

## Texture
- Flat broadcast graphics. No grain, no gradients, no glows.
- A faint 1px horizontal scanline pattern at 3 percent opacity is allowed on footage only.

## Footage
- Shown in a full-width card labeled like a broadcast feed, with a small red source tag in the top-left corner (for example the product's URL).
- Footage may get a quick "LIVE" pulse in the corner but is never recolored.

## Signature moves
- Red wipe: a full-frame red bar sweeps left to right in 300 ms to transition between stories, revealing the next scene behind it.
- Headline slam: the all-caps headline drops in line by line with a hard ease-out and a low hit on the last line; the highlighted word turns yellow 150 ms later.
- Lower-third entrance: rank box snaps in first, then the plate slides out from behind it in 250 ms, staggered 120 ms per row.
- Ticker: the yellow band scrolls continuously at a slow, readable speed, carrying one or two short facts from the story separated by bullets.
- LIVE pulse: the LIVE badge pulses opacity 100 to 70 percent on a 1 second loop.

## Music
- A short, urgent news-sting intro, then a tense pulsing bed around 120 BPM. Stings on headline slams.

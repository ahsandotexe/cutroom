# Style: Slap Wrapped

A deadpan, premium data report. Think an annual report or a year-in-review recap applied to a subject that may be serious or completely ridiculous. The humor, when there is any, comes from the contrast between the calm, expensive look and what is being measured.

## Use it for
- Stories built on a number or a ranking: totals, leaderboards, growth, comparisons, before and after.
- Tech and finance content: AUM, returns, portfolio drift, usage metrics, benchmark results.
- Parody or meme content that should feel grown-up rather than chaotic.

## Avoid it for
- Emotional or human stories with no data at their center.
- Products whose whole appeal is bright and playful, unless the contrast is the point.

## Color tokens
| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #0E0E10 | Every scene |
| Primary text | #F2EEE6 | Headlines, hero numbers, names |
| Secondary text | #A3A3A9 | Labels, supporting lines, captions of lesser weight |
| Inactive | #45454D | Inactive bars, borders, dividers, footage card border |
| Accent | #FF4D2E | The one thing that matters in each scene |

Accent rule: red marks exactly one element per scene. Never two red elements competing in the same frame.

## Type
- Display: Space Grotesk Bold, for hero numbers and headlines.
- Text: Inter (400 and 600), for labels, body and captions.
- Labels: all caps, letter spacing 0.14em, secondary color.
- Numbers: tabular figures.

## Layout
- Left-aligned on an 88px margin at 1080 width, the same in every scene.
- Content group vertically centered in the safe area (see editing-rules.md).
- Generous empty space around the hero, but never an empty half-frame.

## Texture
- Very subtle film grain, around 2 percent, over the whole video so the black never looks flat.
- No gradients, glows, drop shadows or glassmorphism.

## Footage
- Product, app or game footage appears only inside a rounded card with a thin #45454D border on the dark background.
- The footage keeps its own colors. Its look never leaks into the video's own design elements.

## Signature moves
- Hero counter: the key number rolls up from zero over about 1.6 seconds with motion blur and slowing ticks, then lands with a sub hit while a 12px red underline draws left to right in 250 ms.
- Underline match cut: the red underline from one scene slides and stretches into the next scene's first element (often the first chart bar).
- Bar ranking: horizontal bars grow from zero with a 100 ms stagger, widths proportional to value, name above each bar on the left, value on the right in Space Grotesk Bold. All bars start #45454D; the winner flips to red after a half-second music drop.
- Gap bracket: to compare two values, fade everything else to 30 percent opacity and draw a thin red bracket between the two bar ends, with the gap stated in a line below and the number in red.
- End card: the URL or call to action in Space Grotesk Bold, primary color, with the red underline drawing in beneath.

## Music
- Minimal, confident electronic bed around 110 BPM. Dry and understated, never meme music.

## Reference
- First used for the Slap Zaamil "Slap Report" reel (slapzamil.com leaderboard data).

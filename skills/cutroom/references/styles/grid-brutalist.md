# Style: Grid Brutalist

A clean neo-brutalist explainer. Warm grid-paper background, heavy black type, highlighted words in solid color boxes, chunky white cards with hard black offset shadows, and small sticker badges. Friendly, confident and very readable, built for explaining how something works.

## Use it for
- Explainers and breakdowns: how a product works, what went wrong, step-by-step processes.
- Tech, startup and developer content where clarity beats drama.
- Educational series where the same look should repeat across many episodes.

## Avoid it for
- Luxury, premium or very serious finance content, where the playful cards can feel too casual.
- Stories that depend on mood or atmosphere rather than clarity.

## Color tokens
| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #F4F1EA | Every scene (warm off-white) |
| Grid lines | #E4DFD3 | 1px grid, 32px cells at 540 width (64px at 1080) |
| Primary text | #111111 | Headlines, body, card borders and shadows |
| Secondary text | #3A3A3A | Supporting lines |
| Card | #FFFFFF | Content cards |
| Accent | #01411C | Highlight boxes behind key words (white text on it) |
| Highlight | #F5B800 | Sticker badges and the single number or word that matters most (black text on it) |

Accent rule: one green highlight box and at most one mustard highlight per scene. Swap the green for the brand's own primary color when the video is for a specific brand, keeping white text on it at 4.5:1 contrast or better.

## Type
- Display: Archivo Black, for headlines, numbers and badges.
- Text: Inter 500 and 700, for body and card content.
- Labels: Inter 700, all caps, letter spacing 0.1em, in a black pill with off-white text.
- Numbers: tabular figures.

## Layout
- Left-aligned on an 80px margin at 1080 width.
- Content groups vertically centered, as editing-rules.md requires.
- Cards span the content width, 6px black border at 1080 (3px at 540), 12px hard offset shadow at 1080 (6px at 540), no blur, no radius or a small 8px radius used consistently.

## Texture
- The grid is the only texture. No grain, no gradients, no soft shadows.

## Footage
- Product or app footage sits inside a phone or browser frame drawn as a card: white, black border, hard offset shadow.

## Signature moves
- Highlight box: as a key word is spoken, a solid color box wipes in behind it left to right in 200 ms, and the word's color flips to white.
- Card pop: cards scale from 0.92 to 1 with a slight overshoot (ease-out-back) in 300 ms, while the offset shadow slides out from behind them.
- Sticker slap: small badges ("#1", "NEW", "BUG") pop in rotated about 6 degrees with a quick overshoot and a soft click sound.
- Connector lines: simple black arrows draw on between cards to show flow, cause and effect, or before and after.
- Strike-through: a thick red line (#D7263D) draws through a word that is being corrected or disproven, then the replacement appears in a highlight box.

## Music
- Light, upbeat lo-fi or minimal electronic around 100 BPM. Soft clicks and pops on card and sticker entrances.
